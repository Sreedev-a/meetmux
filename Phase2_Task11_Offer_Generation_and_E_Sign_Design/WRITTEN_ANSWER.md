# Offer Generation & E-Sign Design

I began proctoring hardening with a labeled baseline and threshold sweep. The evaluator records the full confusion matrix, false-positive rate, and violation recall at each candidate threshold. Selection minimizes false positives subject to recall of at least 0.80, and the generated report quantifies improvement against the 0.50 baseline. This is explicitly an underway candidate for later validation, not a premature production sign-off.
