# Controlled Cold-Start Evaluation

Four controlled candidates and eleven jobs (ten active/unexpired) were evaluated at K=5. No weights were trained or tuned on these labels. Cold-start NDCG@5 was **0.8446**, Recall@5 **0.7917**, Precision@5 **0.2500**, and MRR **1.0000**, versus baseline 0.4317/0.7083/0.2000/0.4875. NDCG absolute difference: +0.4129; relative difference: +95.66%. This is fixture-based engineering evidence, not retrospective real data or online lift.
