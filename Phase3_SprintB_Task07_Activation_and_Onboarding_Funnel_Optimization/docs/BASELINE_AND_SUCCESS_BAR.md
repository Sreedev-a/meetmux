# Baseline and Success Bar

> A brand-new candidate sees genuinely relevant jobs in their first session.

The inspected Recommendation v1 uses verified skills when present and recency for empty-skill users, but has no dedicated activation evaluation. The defensible comparison implemented here ranks active, unexpired, experience-eligible jobs by popularity, quality and recency. Success controls are 100% non-empty responses when inventory exists, onboarding-only features, valid explanations/metadata, 20% controlled exploration, safe model failure, and eventual production candidate-level relevant-action lift. No business lift target was invented.
