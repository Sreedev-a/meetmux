# First-Session Metrics

Primary production metric: candidates with at least one genuine apply or shortlist in their first session / new candidates receiving recommendations. CTR is supporting, not equivalent to qualified activation. Also track first-session apply/shortlist rates, non-empty coverage, exploration exposure, fallback rate and offline NDCG/Recall.

No genuine first-session cohort exists in the repository, so CTR/apply/shortlist values and behavioural lift are **not available**. `first_session_action_rates.png` states this rather than drawing invented bars.
