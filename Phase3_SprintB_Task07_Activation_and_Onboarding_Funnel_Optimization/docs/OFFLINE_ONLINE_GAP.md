# Offline–Online Gap

On a controlled four-candidate/eleven-job engineering fixture never used to tune weights, popularity baseline NDCG@5 was 0.4317 and cold-start NDCG@5 was 0.8446 (absolute +0.4129; relative +95.66%). Recall@5 changed 0.7083→0.7917. These labels are controlled role/skill relevance checks—not historical behaviour—and do not establish activation or causal online lift.

Production must randomize new candidates, preserve assignment, use onboarding-only features, and compare candidate-level first-session relevant actions with confidence intervals and guardrails.
