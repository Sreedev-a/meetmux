I implemented a Frontend-ready cold-start recommendation service that serves zero-history candidates using verified competencies and onboarding role, location, experience, and employment preferences rather than behavioural history. Active, unexpired, experience-eligible jobs are ranked by an explainable content/competency score, with evidence-based reasons, matched skills, gaps, source, position, and model metadata. A popularity/quality/recency baseline was established, and deterministic 20% exploration introduces eligible discovery items without duplicates. On a controlled held-out engineering fixture of four candidates and eleven jobs, NDCG@5 changed from 0.4317 to 0.8446 and Recall@5 from 0.7083 to 0.7917; these are fixture-based offline proxies, not online activation lift. Genuine first-session outcomes were unavailable, so no CTR, apply, shortlist, or causal lift was fabricated, and a production experiment plan is documented. Seven varied runtime profiles achieved 100% non-empty coverage. Forced model failure returned five explicit `cold-start-fallback-v1` recommendations. The fresh-candidate demo reused Task 6’s exact event logger for positioned impressions, while API and Frontend handoff documentation define safe integration.
