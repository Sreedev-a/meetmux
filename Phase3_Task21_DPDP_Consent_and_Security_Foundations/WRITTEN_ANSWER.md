# DPDP Consent & Security Foundations

I started a privacy-conscious offline fairness audit over recommendation outcomes. The code reports group sample size, selection rate, mean score, qualified-candidate false-negative rate, and disparate-impact ratio, flagging ratios below 0.80 and undersized samples for human review. Sensitive fields are evaluation-only and excluded from ranking. The report explicitly labels the audit underway and documents that small-sample associations require legal, domain, and causal investigation before remediation.
