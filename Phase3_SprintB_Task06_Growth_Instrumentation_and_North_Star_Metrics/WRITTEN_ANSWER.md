I implemented growth instrumentation around the existing PlaceMux ranking service with validated impression, click, apply, and shortlist schemas. Every served result now receives a stable ranking ID, unique impression ID, one-based rank position, model/model-version metadata, UTC timestamp, schema/feature versions, and privacy-minimized context; outcomes retain the originating impression lineage. Events are validated through Pydantic, persisted to an idempotent append-only JSONL store, and analysed for funnels, position bias, and model-version performance. An executed controlled interaction simulation sent 150 requests through the actual instrumented ranker, producing 599 impressions, 169 clicks, 84 applications, and 32 shortlists with 100% position coverage, model-version coverage, schema validity, and outcome joinability. Qualified shortlist rate per ranked impression was defined as the design-choice north star, supported by CTR, apply, reliability, fallback, and data-quality guardrails. One full impression-to-shortlist journey was reconstructed. Intentional model failure safely produced explicit `fallback-v1` events, and Backend/Data Analyst handoff documentation defines integration, joins, idempotency, privacy, and analysis. No production traffic or online lift is claimed.
